HNK brand model, second face (owner decision, 2026-09-19).

The owner sent four candidate faces and chose B, C and D — one woman in a silver
Hmong-style headdress. A, the fourth, is the OUTGOING face: the Freeform hero that
../hnk-model.jpg has carried since 5.62.1. B/C/D are three views of the same woman,
which is what the old README asked for ("one three-quarter crop alone let the face
drift", run #10).

PROVENANCE, stated plainly: these came from the owner, who says they are their own
studio's AI generations of a synthetic character — not a photograph of a real person.
That is the owner's attestation; it was not independently verified here. The owner was
told, before these were committed, that this repository is public and that committing
them publishes the source files permanently, and chose to commit them (2026-09-19).

The supplied files were 360px wide (chat downscales). They are stored here upscaled
2x LANCZOS, uncropped: a 360px source has too few pixels to crop to the face. The
headdress, earrings, collar and sky are excluded in the PROMPT, not in the frame —
see tools/face_swap_jobs*.json, which tells the model to copy the face and nothing else.

Card art generated from these is the studio's own work and is public by design
(docs/ is the published site). 589 of the 642 card pictures under docs/app/lib/wf
carry the brand model; 50 carry no person and 3 carry someone else (two newborn
pictures and a child) and are left alone.
